# STAR story bank

Interview answers in STAR form (Situation, Task, Action, Result), 1-2 minutes each. Every fact here
comes from facts.md. Interview prep picks stories by the "Use for" tags. When a new story or fact
comes up in prep, Claude adds it here and to facts.md, only with your OK.

Lines marked `[ask]` still need your own answer.

---

### 1. Story title (what it shows)
**S:** The situation, in one or two sentences.
**T:** What you had to do.
**A:** What you did, step by step (your actions, not the team's).
**R:** The result, with a real number if there is one.
**Use for:** "tell me about a time...", "a project you're proud of", ...

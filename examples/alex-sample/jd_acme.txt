Acme Analytics is hiring a Data Analyst to join our Commercial Insights team.

About the role
You will turn sales and customer data into dashboards and recommendations for our commercial leaders.

What you'll do
- Build and maintain Tableau dashboards for sales and marketing.
- Write SQL to answer questions from stakeholders across the business.
- Define KPIs and help teams use them consistently.
- Design and analyze A/B tests for pricing and promotions.
- Improve data quality checks in our pipelines.

What you bring
- 2-4 years of experience in a data analyst role.
- Strong SQL and Excel.
- Experience with Tableau or Power BI.
- Python (pandas) is an asset.
- Experience with dbt or Looker is nice to have.
- Clear communication with non-technical stakeholders.

Location: Chicago, IL (hybrid, 2 days a week in office)
Salary range: $85,000 - $105,000 USD per year
